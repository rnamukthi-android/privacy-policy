# Rnamukthi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

## Introduction

Rnamukthi keeps your financial data on your phone and sends it nowhere. Rnamukthi is an Android app that helps individual borrowers in India plan repayment across several loans. It is a planning tool only: it is not a lender, and it does not give financial advice.

The app is published by Vaibhav, an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Rnamukthi Android app, including pilot (test) versions distributed through Google Play testing tracks.

## At a glance

You do not need an account, and the app never reads your SMS, bank accounts, contacts or location.

| What | Where it is kept | Leaves your phone? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your name, income, expenses, loans, payments and plans | Only on your phone | No |
| Advertising IDs, device identifiers, analytics, crash reports | Not collected | Never |

The app has no permission to use the internet and makes no network connections. We do not collect, sell or share your information, and we show no ads.

## Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it. That includes:

- **Profile:** the name you enter, monthly income, monthly fixed expenses, financial goal, preferred repayment strategy, planned extra payment, and app settings (theme, reminder preferences).
- **Loans:** loan name, lender name, loan type, amounts (principal, outstanding balance, EMI, credit limit, statement and minimum due), interest rate, tenure, due and statement days, security and prepayment details, and any notes you type.
- **Income:** income sources, amounts, frequency, expected dates, and which income you have marked as received.
- **Payments and borrowing:** payments you record against a loan, and amounts you borrow again on a credit card, overdraft or informal loan.
- **Plans and activity:** extra-payment plans, plan adjustments, and an activity log of what you added, changed or archived.

The app does not ask for bank account numbers, card numbers, PAN, Aadhaar or passwords. Please do not type them into the free-text notes fields.

The financial database and plan data are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so they are not copied to your Google account. The one setting that may be backed up is whether the app runs in its Lite or Full mode.

## What we do not collect

- No accounts, logins, phone numbers or email addresses.
- No access to SMS, bank accounts, UPI, credit bureaus or other financial apps. Every figure is typed in by you.
- No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- No advertising ID or other device identifiers.
- No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The app contains no third-party tracking code.
- No data at all leaves your phone, so we hold no copy of anything you enter.

## Permissions

The app asks for one Android permission:

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Reminders about due dates, statements and expected income. They are created on your phone; no push service is used. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings. |

The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

## How we use information

On your phone, the app uses your data only to calculate repayment plans and show reminders. We do not receive it, so we do not use it for credit decisions, lending, marketing, advertising or profiling.

## Sharing

We do not receive your information, so we have nothing to sell, rent, trade or share with advertisers, lenders, credit bureaus or data brokers.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. **Settings > Reset all local data** erases everything the app holds. Uninstalling the app does the same. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Security

On your phone, data sits in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Protect it with a screen lock. If you lose or share your phone, or uninstall without a backup of your own, the data on it may be lost or visible to whoever has the phone. The app has no cloud backup and no restore feature.

## Your rights

We hold none of your personal data, so there is nothing for us to give access to, correct or erase. You control your data on your phone and can edit or delete it in the app at any time. If you have a question or grievance about how the app handles data, contact us (see Contact). If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## Children

Rnamukthi is meant for adults managing their own loans and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app changes how it handles data, for example if a feature that sends data off your phone is added. The date at the top shows the latest version. For a significant change, we will also tell you in the app or on the Google Play listing before it takes effect.
